02 / RIGHTS

Know what the law says.

Start with these core rights. For legal decisions or protection concerns, seek help from a qualified professional.

01

Education

Every child has the right to basic education.

02

Protection

Children must be protected from abuse, neglect and violence.

03

Health

Every child has a right to the highest attainable standard of health.

04

Participation

Your views matter in decisions affecting you, according to your age and maturity.
