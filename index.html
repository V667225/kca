Your Rights as a Kenyan Child

Right to Education

Every child has the right to free and compulsory basic education. (Article 53)

Right to Health

You have the right to the highest attainable standard of health and medical care.

Right to Protection

You must be protected from abuse, neglect, and all forms of violence.

The Law is on Your Side

In Kenya, your rights are protected by the Constitution (Article 53) and the Children Act 2022. These laws ensure that anyone who hurts you or denies you your rights faces justice.

What happens to offenders?

  • • Abuse & Neglect: Offenders can face heavy fines and long prison sentences.
  • • Cyberbullying/Online Abuse: Up to 10 years in prison or a fine of 2 million KES.
  • • Inheritance Theft: Anyone who tries to take property belonging to a child can be jailed for 2 years.
  • • Corporal Punishment: Physical beating of children is strictly illegal in all settings.
